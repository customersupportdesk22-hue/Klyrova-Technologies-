GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Helpful answers to the things clients usually want to know.

Do I need to pay the full amount before work starts?

No. Our suggested milestone plan is 40% to begin, 30% at the agreed build-review stage and 30% before launch or final handover. The exact schedule is written in your quotation.

Do I need to have a domain and hosting already?

No. We can help you choose and set them up. We recommend that the domain and hosting accounts are registered in your name, with provider charges paid separately.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on the scope, how quickly content and feedback are provided, and any integrations. We confirm a realistic timeline after discussing your requirements.

Can you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. We can discuss projects remotely, quote in USD $for international work and agree communication, milestones and suitable payment arrangements before starting.

Can you add M-Pesa, card payments or other payment providers?

We can assess and integrate a suitable provider where the provider supports your country, business type and required features. The client normally owns the merchant account and settlement details. Provider approval, fees and compliance requirements are separate.

What happens after the website goes live?

We provide the agreed handover and can offer ongoing maintenance, updates and support under a separate plan. Hosting, domain renewal and third-party subscriptions remain recurring costs.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Share the website link and explain what is not working or what you want to improve. We'll review the scope before recommending changes.

Can an AI assistant respond when my business is closed?

Yes. We can build an AI assistant to answer approved frequently asked questions, guide visitors and collect contact details at any time. It should identify itself as an AI assistant and direct sensitive or complex requests to your team.

Can you help us get a bulk SMS Sender ID?

We can help you choose a supported messaging provider, prepare the required business details and submit the application. Sender ID approval, availability, charges and messaging rules are determined by the provider and applicable requirements; approval is not guaranteed.

Can you add customer reviews to our website?

Yes. We can add a review collection flow, display genuine customer feedback and link to supported review platforms. We do not create or publish fabricated customer reviews.
